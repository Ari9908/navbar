Find your pet page!


Dog


This is a dog! Dogs are "Man's best friend for a reason! They are typically loyal, and some are affectionate. They teach you about unconditional love, and serve as amazing emotional support. There are tons of different breeds of dog to choose from based on if you want them working, showing, or just as a companion. Dogs are not easy work though. They can cost $2000 or more! They're also a time commitment, and require attention, regular grooming, food, exercise, and more. Is a dog right for you?

Cat

This is a cat. They're fluffy, sometimes cuddly, and super independent and low maintenence. You only need to keep them fed and watered. They are incredibly intelligent and self-sustaining, so they sometimes go on walks by themselves. They also know how to groom themselves. There are many different kinds of cats to choose from! Some are fluffier and some have longer hair and they also come in many different colors. Is a cat right for you?

Bunny

This is a bunny. They are fluffy, energetic, and full of personality. They can be affectionate. They require adequete space to run around, and usually get more than one becasue they are social creatures. They need an enclosure to sleep and lay in, as well as hay 24/7 and food. They also need water. But never bathe them because it is bad for them, so you should always make sure that you groom them properly. Is a bunny right for you?

Fish

This is a fish. They are cool to look at and relatively cheap depending on how many you have. If you would like a cool pet that respects boundaries and personal space, you should consider a fish. There are many different kinds of fish, like beta and goldfish which are low maintenance and don't require as much space. However, if you would like to get more creative, you can certainly start an aquarium! Are fish the right pets for you?

Bird

This is a bird! Birds are unique companions that are vocal and can be friendly. They are very intelligent and some, like parrots, can learn to speak! There are many different birds to choose from, like chickens, ducks, or parakeets. Are birds right for you?

Turtle

This is a turtle! Turtles are slow moving reptiles with cool shells! There are a bunch of different turtles from red eared slidrs that are underwater to land tortoises like Hermann's tortoises. They make a great long term pet and some can live for 50+ years.

Axolotl

This is an Axolotl. They are a kind of salamander. They are omnivores and live in water. They come in a few colors, like speckled, pink, and black. They do require adaquate spacing, and are a little high maintenance. But they are asuper adorable. Is an axolotl right for you?

Spider

This is a spider! There are all different kinds of spiders to suit your needs. If you're looking for a small cute spider, you can get a jumping spider. Or, if you're looking for something to scare your friends, try a tarantula! Is a spider right for you?
